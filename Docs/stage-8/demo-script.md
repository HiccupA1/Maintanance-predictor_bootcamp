# Demo Script

1. Introduce the single-plant predictive-maintenance MVP.
2. Switch to Admin and register a critical asset.
3. Switch to PlantManager and configure a temperature threshold.
4. Switch to Operator and enter a normal reading, then an inclusive-boundary breach.
5. Show the alert context, priority, and deduplication behavior.
6. Convert the alert into a work order.
7. Switch to MaintenanceEngineer, add a parts line, and close the work order.
8. Verify the resolved alert and updated service date.
9. Show `/docs`, structured errors, and the current release-readiness status.
10. Call out deferred authentication, notifications, dashboards, and ML prediction.
